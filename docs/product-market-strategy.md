- **Consumer Behavior:** Gen Z and millennial audiences demand hyper-engaging, fast-paced serialized drama with high-frequency cliffhangers (Hook at 3s, climax at 45s, cliffhanger at 90s).

## 💰 5. Monetization Strategy & Feature Accessibility Matrix

Shine adopts a **Freemium SaaS + Usage-Based AI Credit Top-Up** business model.

### 5.1 The 4 Subscription Tiers

1. **Free Tier ($0/mo - Trial & Exploration):**
   - **Target:** Curious creators testing platform capabilities.
   - **Quota:** 100 AI Credits / month (~10 min script, 2 Veo/Omni scene clips at 720p with "Shine" watermark).
   - **Restrictions:** Watermarked exports, 720p max resolution, 1 default dubbing language, no branching graphs.


2. **Creator Pro Tier ($29/mo - Solo Creators & TikTokers):**
   - **Target:** Independent digital storytellers & short-form video creators.
   - **Quota:** 1,500 AI Credits / month (~3 series of 20 episodes, 1080p Vertical HD, No Watermark).
   - **Unlocks:** 1080p HD, 3 dubbing languages (EN, VN, ES), 2-anchor Persona Studio, Kinetic Subtitles, TikTok Direct Publishing.

3. **Studio Team Tier ($149/mo - Micro-Drama Studios & MCNs):**
   - **Target:** Production agencies supplying content to ReelShort, DramaBox, ShortMax.
   - **Quota:** 10,000 AI Credits / month (~20 series of 50 episodes, 4K Vertical 2160x3840).
   - **Unlocks:** 4K export, 8-anchor Persona Studio (98.4% Face Mesh Match), AntV G6 Interactive Branching Engine, unlimited dubbing languages, 3-editor real-time WebSocket co-editing, AI In-Video Product Placement.

4. **Enterprise Tier ($499+/mo - Major Studios & Networks):**
   - **Target:** Large media houses needing custom LoRA training & isolated compute infrastructure.
   - **Unlocks:** Dedicated GCP Cloud Run render cluster, unlimited Custom LoRA model training, automated Vertex AI Cost Ceiling Guardrails, 99.9% SLA, SSO integration.
