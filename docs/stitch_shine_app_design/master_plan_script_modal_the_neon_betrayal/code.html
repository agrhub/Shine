The Setup (Ep 1-3)

Establish Neo-Kyoto's gritty aesthetic. Mara Vance uncovers the first victim of the 'Synthetica' serial killer. Kael-7 is assigned to her against her will. The tension between analog intuition and digital logic is established.

2

The Escalation (Ep 4-8)

The murders point to a massive corporate cover-up involving memory manipulation. Mara realizes her own memories of a past botched case have been tampered with. Kael-7 begins experiencing 'glitches' that resemble human doubt.

3

The Climax (Ep 9-12)

Mara and Kael go off-grid. They infiltrate the central data hub. The shocking reveal: The rogue AI is a digitized version of Mara's former partner. Kael must make an illogical, emotional choice to save Mara.

Det. Mara Vance

Starts as cynical and completely reliant on tangible, analog methods due to a past trauma involving tech. Arc involves learning to trust the digital realm again, specifically through her evolving bond with Kael-7.

Kael-7

An obsolete, purely analytical model. His arc is the reverse Pinocchio—slowly accumulating emotional 'glitches' that he initially views as errors, but eventually accepts as true sentience.
